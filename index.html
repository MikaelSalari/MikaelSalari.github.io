About

I'm a Software Engineer at Atlas Copco with over 12 years of experience and a PhD in Computer Science. Within the Software Test Office in Stockholm, I focus on designing and executing product tests, improving testing processes, and ensuring high-quality software delivery. My strengths lie in test automation, test case design, and building smart, efficient workflows that align with organizational goals.

Before joining Atlas Copco, I worked as a PhD researcher across several EU and international research projects, collaborating with major automation firms such as ABB and Alstom. My research specialized in test automation and reliability for industrial embedded systems and PLC-based signaling software — developing test strategies, reusable test harnesses, and conducting defect analyses that resulted in publications at international conferences and journals.

Earlier in my career, I held several IT engineering and technical support roles in Iran. I'm passionate about advancing software robustness through innovative testing methodologies and automation-driven quality improvement.
